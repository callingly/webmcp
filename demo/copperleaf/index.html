How it works

Three steps, and you can stop after any of them.

  1. Send us twelve months of bills. We size the system from real usage, not a guess off satellite imagery.
  2. Get a written quote. Installed price, production estimate, payback period. No pressure, no expiring discount.
  3. Book the install. Most jobs are one day on the roof, plus utility interconnection.

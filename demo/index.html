This page registers two WebMCP tools. An AI agent browsing it can check when the sales team is actually free and — with your say-so — have a rep call you within a minute or two.

Try it

  1. Open this page in ChatGPT's in-app browser, or Chrome 149+ with chrome://flags/#enable-webmcp-testing enabled.
  2. Ask your agent one of these.
  3. Confirm the call when the page asks.
"Is the sales team available to talk right now?"
"When is the earliest I could speak to someone?"
"Have someone call me on +1 415 555 0134 about pricing."
"I need a human on the line, not a chatbot."
